How MathJod Workflow Works
  1. Click New Equation to open the interactive MathLive editor dialog.
  2. When inserted, the equation is rendered into a crisp picture object with its LaTeX source invisibly saved in the image's Alt Text.
  3. To edit later, simply click the equation image in Word: the Edit Math button will automatically activate!
